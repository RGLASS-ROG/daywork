Privacy policy

In short

Daywork has no server and no user accounts of its own. Your tasks are stored on your device and, if you choose, in your own Microsoft OneDrive. The developer cannot see your data. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your lists, tasks, notes, reminders and settings are saved in your browser's or app's local storage on your device. They never leave your device unless you sign in with Microsoft (see below) or download a backup file yourself. Uninstalling the app or clearing its site data deletes them.

Optional sync with Microsoft OneDrive and Outlook

If you sign in with your Microsoft account, Daywork connects directly from your device to Microsoft (Microsoft Graph). It requests these permissions:

  • Sign-in (openid, profile): your name and e-mail address are shown in the settings and kept locally on your device.
  • Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder: Daywork creates the folder Apps/Daywork in your OneDrive and stores your tasks there in a file named daywork.json. It cannot access any other files.
  • Calendars.ReadWrite (only if you switch on “Add to Outlook calendar”): each reminder is added to your Outlook calendar as a short event and removed again when the task is completed or deleted.

This data is transferred only between your device and Microsoft. It does not pass through any server operated by the developer. Microsoft processes it as the provider of your account under its own terms: Microsoft Privacy Statement. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the features you request). You can revoke access at any time at account.live.com/consent/Manage and delete the Apps/Daywork folder in OneDrive.

Hosting

The app's files are delivered by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When the files are loaded, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, date and time and browser type to deliver the pages and protect its service. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing the app securely). GitHub participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Daywork uses your device's built-in fonts and contacts no other third-party services.

App stores

If you install Daywork from an app store (Microsoft Store, Google Play, Apple App Store), the store operator processes data such as your account and download information under its own privacy policy. The developer only receives anonymous, aggregated statistics from the store, if any.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are created on your device by your browser or operating system. No push service is used.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Because the developer stores no personal data about you, you can exercise most of these rights directly: export or delete your data in the app, delete the OneDrive folder, or revoke the Microsoft permissions. For questions, contact the e-mail address above.

Daten auf deinem Gerät

Listen, Aufgaben, Notizen, Erinnerungen und Einstellungen werden im lokalen Speicher deines Browsers bzw. der App auf deinem Gerät gespeichert. Sie verlassen das Gerät nur, wenn du dich mit Microsoft anmeldest (siehe unten) oder selbst eine Sicherungsdatei herunterlädst. Wenn du die App deinstallierst oder ihre Websitedaten löschst, sind sie entfernt.

Optionaler Abgleich mit Microsoft OneDrive und Outlook

Wenn du dich mit deinem Microsoft-Konto anmeldest, verbindet sich Daywork direkt von deinem Gerät aus mit Microsoft (Microsoft Graph). Dabei werden folgende Berechtigungen angefragt:

  • Anmeldung (openid, profile): Name und E-Mail-Adresse werden in den Einstellungen angezeigt und lokal auf deinem Gerät gespeichert.
  • Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder: Daywork legt in deinem OneDrive den Ordner Apps/Daywork an und speichert deine Aufgaben dort in der Datei daywork.json. Auf andere Dateien hat die App keinen Zugriff.
  • Calendars.ReadWrite (nur wenn du „In Outlook-Kalender eintragen“ einschaltest): Jede Erinnerung wird als kurzer Termin in deinen Outlook-Kalender eingetragen und wieder entfernt, wenn die Aufgabe erledigt oder gelöscht wird.

Diese Daten werden nur zwischen deinem Gerät und Microsoft übertragen, nicht über einen Server des Entwicklers. Microsoft verarbeitet sie als Anbieter deines Kontos nach eigenen Bedingungen: Microsoft-Datenschutzerklärung. Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO (Bereitstellung der von dir gewünschten Funktionen). Den Zugriff kannst du jederzeit unter account.live.com/consent/Manage widerrufen und den Ordner Apps/Daywork in OneDrive löschen.
